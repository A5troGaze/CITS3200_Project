# Sprint 3 Team Agreement

This agreement sets out roles and expectations for Sprint 3. It is intended to give the team a clear, shared standard of work, and a fair basis for raising concerns if that standard isn't met.

## Group Member Expectations

- **All** team members are to be on time to meetings.
- **All** team members must aim to be on time submitting their timesheets. Do not make life difficult for the Project Manager, who is responsible for submitting these.
- **All** team members are to contribute during group discussions.

## Developer Expectations

- Developers are to stay on track and finish work in a timely manner.
- Developers are to keep testers up to date at all times.
- Developers must comment their code to make it readable for testers.
- Developers should finish their code and development by **4th October** at the latest, in order to give testers sufficient time to test, debug, and ask questions, etc.

## Tester Expectations

- Testers are to raise dire concerns **AS SOON** as they pop up
- Testers are to debug to the best of their ability
- Testers are to clearly document their testing and produce something similar/in line with the project acceptance tests from Sprint 1
